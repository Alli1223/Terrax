## Clothing

Worn armour is **painted onto the player/NPC rig** (clothing_painter.cpp),
so it has no free-standing footprint; the world-drop pickup is a fixed
6×4×6 "folded garment" coloured by the item's palette. Pieces are
generated per **slot** × **material tier**, gated by role:

- **Slots:** Helmet, Shoulders, Chest, Legs, Feet (+ Main-hand / Off-hand for weapons).
- **Tiers:** Cloth (DPS/Healer), Leather (DPS), Plate (Tank) — and anything lighter.
- **Rarity:** Common / Rare / Legendary — scales stats + adds runes/gems/glow.
